# Health insurance

## What it is
Health insurance pays for hospitalisation and related medical expenses up to the sum insured. Policies can cover an individual or a whole family under one shared limit (a family floater). Cashless treatment is usually available at network hospitals; elsewhere the client pays first and claims reimbursement.

## Why employer cover is often not enough
Group health cover from an employer usually ends when the job ends, may have a low sum insured, and does not continue into retirement. Buying a personal policy while young and healthy helps because pre-existing conditions and age can raise premiums or add waiting periods later. Medical costs tend to rise faster than general inflation, so the sum insured should be reviewed periodically.

## Waiting periods and limits
Most policies have an initial waiting period (often 30 days for illnesses other than accidents), specific waiting periods for listed treatments, and a longer waiting period for pre-existing diseases. Some policies cap room rent or charge co-payments, especially for senior citizens. These terms vary by insurer and product and should be read in the policy wording.

## Top-up and super top-up plans
A top-up or super top-up plan pays only after expenses cross a chosen deductible. It is a cost-effective way to raise total cover: for example, a base policy plus a super top-up with a deductible equal to the base sum insured.

## Senior citizens
Older clients face higher premiums and stricter underwriting. Planning health cover before retirement, and checking renewal terms, co-payments and coverage of pre-existing conditions, is especially important for clients aged 56 and above.

## Frequently asked questions
**Is health insurance needed if the client has large investments?** A serious illness can force selling investments at a bad time; insurance protects the long-term plan.
**Does health insurance have a tax benefit?** Premiums may qualify for a deduction under prevailing income-tax rules; the limits change over time and should be checked for the current year.
