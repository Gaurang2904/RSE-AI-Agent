# Practice policy: data handling and advice boundaries (synthetic example policy)

## Client data stays local
Client records are personal financial data. They are processed only on the practice's own computers, are never uploaded to external AI services, and are never emailed or shared without a documented business need and the client's consent. Contact details are not loaded into analytics tools.

## Educational information versus advice
Analytics tools and knowledge articles provide educational information and internal insights, such as which clients lack term cover. They do not provide personalised financial advice. In India, personalised investment advice is regulated by SEBI and insurance advice and distribution by IRDAI; recommendations for a specific client must come from an appropriately qualified and registered professional after a suitability assessment.

## How the assistant should answer
The assistant explains concepts, cites the knowledge articles it used, reports facts from client data, and flags review triggers. It does not tell a client to buy or sell a specific product or security. When a question asks for a recommendation, it explains the relevant considerations and suggests an adviser review.

## Retention
Working copies of client data and generated reports are deleted when no longer needed.
