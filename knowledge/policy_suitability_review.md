# Practice policy: client suitability review (synthetic example policy)

## Purpose
This internal policy describes when a client's profile and portfolio must be reviewed so that guidance stays suitable. It is a synthetic example used by this demo, not a real firm's policy.

## Annual review
Every client receives a review at least once a year covering goals, risk profile, protection cover and product holdings. Reviews are recorded in the CRM as follow-up tasks.

## Triggers for an immediate review
A review must be scheduled within 30 days when any of the following applies:
- the client is aged 56 or above and has a Moderately Aggressive or Aggressive risk profile;
- the client has one or more dependants but no term insurance;
- the client has no health insurance;
- the client has no financial goals mapped;
- the client reports a major life event (marriage, birth, job change, retirement, new loan).

## Protection-first rule
Before proposing higher-risk products (PMS, LAS, aggressive equity), advisers confirm that the client has an emergency fund and adequate term and health cover, or that the client has declined such cover in writing.

## Documentation
Each review records the facts considered, the guidance given, and the client's decision. Product recommendations are made only after a documented suitability assessment by a qualified adviser.
